
# How to ask help from Chatgpt?

## Prompt to keep
```
Assume role of expert resume writer, help me customize my current roles and responsibilities as per the job sescription. I am applying for entry level IT Support roles in Australia so that my resume passes the ATS level and recruiters scan to get the interview call. 
I want the content to be in simple English and humanized. I want output to be in bullet points neither too lengthy nor too short making sure we cover the important points.
```
- After pasting this prompt paste the job decription from website
    - Write JD and paste following
        - Desired Skills and Experience
        - Preferred skills and experience
        - This role will include
    
- Now go to resume and copy paste current role typing
    - Current role: paste content here

- Since current role should be in present continuous. So, say to Chatgpt
    - Convert it into present continuous tense
